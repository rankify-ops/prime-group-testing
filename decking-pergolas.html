FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about decking and pergolas in Melbourne.

How much does a deck or pergola cost in Melbourne?

Costs vary based on the size of the structure, materials chosen, and the condition of your site. We provide a detailed fixed-price quote after an on-site assessment so you know the full cost upfront. Contact us for a free assessment and obligation-free quote.

Do I need a permit for a deck or pergola?

It depends on the height, size and location of the structure. Many smaller decks and pergolas don't require a permit, but larger or elevated structures usually do. We advise you on permit requirements during the quoting stage and manage any applications on your behalf.

Timber or composite decking — which is better?

Timber decking offers a natural look and can be more affordable upfront, while composite decking costs more initially but requires less ongoing maintenance and resists fading, splintering and rot. We'll talk you through both options during your consultation so you can choose what suits your home and budget.

How long does it take to build a deck or pergola?

Most decks and pergolas take between 1 to 3 weeks to build, depending on size and complexity. We provide a clear schedule before work begins and keep you updated on progress throughout the build.

Do decks and pergolas need much maintenance?

Timber structures benefit from periodic oiling or staining to protect against the weather, while composite decking and aluminium pergolas need very little upkeep. We'll advise on the right maintenance routine for the materials used on your project.
