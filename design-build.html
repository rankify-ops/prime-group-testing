All In-House

One Team from Design to Completion

Northside Building Co now offers a complete design and build service — all managed in-house, from first concept through to handover. Whether you're planning a bathroom or kitchen renovation, a granny flat or secondary dwelling, or a full home modification project, our team handles every stage under one roof.

No juggling separate designers, draftspeople, and builders — just one point of contact, one team, and one seamless process from design to completion. As a licensed registered builder and NDIS-registered provider, Northside brings the same all-inclusive approach to home modification projects — making the process simpler and faster for clients who need funded, compliant builds.

  • Initial consultation and site assessment
  • Concept design and drafting
  • Council and planning approvals (including VC253 exemption pathway advice for secondary dwellings)
  • Fixed-price quoting — no hidden costs
  • Construction delivery by our licensed, experienced team
  • Ongoing project management from start to finish
How It Works

The Design & Build Process

From your initial idea to walking through the finished project — here's how we make it happen.

1. Consultation & Site Assessment

We visit your property, discuss your vision, and assess the site to understand what's possible within your budget and council requirements.

2. Design & Drafting

Our in-house team produces concept designs and working drawings tailored to your brief — no outsourcing, no delays.

3. Approvals & Permits

We handle all council and planning approvals, including VC253 exemption pathway advice for secondary dwellings. You don't have to chase paperwork.

4. Construction & Handover

Our licensed team builds your project with ongoing project management from start to finish. Fixed-price quoting means no surprises along the way.
