Registered Builder • Camberwell

Premium Craftsmanship for Camberwell's Period Homes

Camberwell is home to some of Melbourne's finest Edwardian and Victorian residences — homes with ornate plasterwork, leadlight windows, tessellated tile verandahs, and timber detailing that demands a builder with genuine heritage experience. Northside Building Co has spent over 15 years renovating and extending these homes, working within Boroondara's heritage overlay requirements to create results that respect the original architecture while delivering the open-plan layouts, modern kitchens, and luxury bathrooms that Camberwell families expect. Our work near Camberwell Junction and throughout the broader suburb reflects a deep appreciation for period craftsmanship.

Beyond heritage renovations, we build granny flats in Camberwell's established rear gardens, fit out retail and professional tenancies around Camberwell Junction and Burke Road, and install NDIS accessibility modifications that integrate seamlessly with period interiors. As a registered builder and NDIS registered supplier, we handle heritage permits, Boroondara council approvals, and complex multi-trade coordination so you can focus on enjoying the finished result.
