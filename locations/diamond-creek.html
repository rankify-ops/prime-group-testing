Registered Builder • Diamond Creek

Building for Diamond Creek's Green Wedge Lifestyle

Diamond Creek sits within Nillumbik Shire's prized green wedge, where established family homes on generous blocks back onto bushland and the Diamond Creek Trail. Building here demands a builder who knows the area — from bushfire attack level assessments and BAL-compliant construction to working with the environmental significance overlays that protect the Shire's natural character. Northside Building Co has been delivering exactly that for over 17 years.

Whether you want to add a self-contained dwelling to your large Diamond Creek block, modernise a dated kitchen in an established home near the creek, or need urgent storm-damage repairs after wild weather rolls through the ranges, we handle it all. As an NDIS registered supplier, we also carry out accessibility upgrades for participants across the Nillumbik corridor. Get in touch for a free, no-obligation quote.

  • Over 17 years as a registered domestic & commercial builder
  • Secondary dwellings & self-contained units on larger lots
  • Full home renovations — kitchens, wet areas & open-plan living
  • Shopfront & commercial premises fit-outs
  • Accessible home modifications for NDIS participants
  • Emergency storm & weather-damage repairs, 24/7
  • End-to-end project management for new builds & extensions
